1. Play Field & Rows

Each player commands a Front Row (battle line) and Back Row (support, capitals, buildings), deploying Champions and Tactics while protecting their Leader/Capital.

2. Card Types & Traits

Capitals (Back Row, Heritage Tokens), Champions (enter unprepared, heal at end of turn), Buildings (limit 1 per turn, cannot move), and Tactics (one-time effects).

3. Deck Setup & Phases

Max 1 Leader per deck, max 3 copies (1 for Unique), 1 Faction + Universal. Turn phases: Draw → Play → Fight → Move.

4. Winning Conditions

Steel Victory (defeat enemy Leader/Capital), Legend Victory (Capital Heritage Tokens), and Silk Victory (10+ resources in Draw phase for 3 Silk Tokens).

5. Keywords & Positions

Breach (carry damage through), Ranged, Pacifist, Reincarnate, Unique • Positions: Adjacent, In Front Of, Behind, Beside.
