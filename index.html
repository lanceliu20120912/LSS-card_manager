Legends of Steel and Silk — Official Rulebook

Complete Game Rules, Card Types, Turn Phases, Keywords & Victory Conditions

1. What is Legends of Steel and Silk? & The Play Field

Legends of Steel and Silk (LSS) is a collectible card game for two or more players. Each player selects a Leader or Capital and builds a board full of champions and buildings. You can win by destroying the enemy's leader/capital, obtaining a high amount of resources, or building a great capital.

Two-Row Board Layout

Each player has two rows (Front Row and Back Row, 5 slots each) separated by the Battle Line, plus a face-down Deck (minimum 15 cards) and a Discard Pile.

Some cards may move between rows during the Move phase, while others (such as Capitals and Buildings) remain locked on the row where they are played.

Resource Cap System

Players have a resource cap that limits how many cards they can maintain in play. Your cap is determined by their starting Leader or Capital, Buildings, and other in-game effects.

Champions hold onto their resource cost while in play until they leave play or you gain more resources.

2. Card Types & Traits

Capitals Back Row Only • Max 1

Represent the center of your civilization (e.g., Mohenjo-Daro: +5 Res, 0 ATK, 6 DEF, Condition: Play a Shudra or Vaisya, Threshold: 5).

  • Starting with a Capital grants its resource bonus (e.g., +5) and represents you as the player.
  • Playing a Capital from hand grants no resource bonus, costs 0, counts as your 1 building for that turn, and must be placed on the back row.
  • Cannot move between rows and cannot attack.
  • Heritage Tokens: Meeting the Capital's Condition places 1 Heritage Token on it (+1 Defense each). Reaching its Threshold wins the game!
Champions Enters Unprepared

Represent the people who work and fight for your civilization (e.g., Sunzi: Cost 4, 3 ATK, 3 DEF, Champion - Scholar, Unique).

  • Played during the Play phase; may fight in Fight phase and move in Move phase.
  • Play any number per turn up to your resource cap.
  • Heals at end of turn: Unlike Leaders or Capitals, damaged Champions restore full defense at the end of the Move phase.
  • Subtypes (e.g., Scholar, Kshatriya, Shudra, Vaisya) trigger synergy abilities.
Buildings Limit 1 Per Turn

Structures and monuments created by your civilization (e.g., Irrigation System: Neutral, +1 Res, 0 ATK, 1 DEF).

  • Usually cost 0 resources and often increase your resource cap (e.g., +1).
  • Each player may only play one Building (or Capital) per turn during the Play phase.
  • Remain locked on the row where they are played and cannot move.
Tactics 0 Cost • One-Time Effect

One-time effects played when a specific condition is met (e.g., Horse Sacrifice: India, Condition: It is your play phase, Effect: Friendly Kshatriya get +1/+0 this turn and must attack).

  • Do not require resources to play.
  • When its Condition is met, reveal it from your hand to resolve its Effect, then place it in your discard pile.

3. Deck Setup & The Four Turn Phases

Pre-Game Setup & Deck Rules
  • Construct a main deck of at least 15 cards (excluding your starting Leader or Capital).
  • Faction Rule: You may ONLY include cards that match your Leader or Capital's faction, plus Neutral / Universal cards.
  • Copy & Leader Limits: Max 3 copies of any standard card; max 1 copy of any Unique card; max 1 Leader per deck.
  • Start with 1 Leader or Capital in play (choose front or back row). The player whose starting card has the lower resource score goes first.
  • After placing your starting Leader/Capital, each player draws 4 cards.
Phase 1: DRAW
  • Draw 1 card from your deck (skip draw on Round 1 if going first).
  • Prepare all your unprepared champions (turn upright).
  • If you have 10+ resources, gain 1 Silk Token.
Phase 2: PLAY
  • Play up to 1 Building or Capital per turn.
  • Play any number of Champions within your resource cap.
  • New Champions enter play unprepared (turned sideways; cannot attack or move this turn).
Phase 3: FIGHT
  • Attack with prepared Champions and/or Leader.
  • Must target enemy front row first unless empty or attacker has Ranged.
  • Non-Ranged attackers must be in your front row (unless your front row is empty).
  • Attacker and target deal simultaneous Strength damage to each other's Defense; 0 Defense dies.
Phase 4: MOVE
  • Move prepared Champions and/or Leader between rows or reorder slots on their current row.
  • Damaged Champions and Buildings heal to full defense (Leaders & Capitals do not heal).
  • If you have 3+ Silk Tokens, you win!

4. Three Ways to Win the Game

Steel Victory

Reduce your opponent's Leader or Capital on their rows to 0 Defense and you immediately win the game.

Legend Victory

Start with or play a Capital. Each time you meet its Condition, place 1 Heritage Token (+1 DEF) on it. Reach the Capital's Threshold to win.

Silk Victory

During your Draw phase, if you have 10+ resources, gain 1 Silk Token (dropping below 10 removes all Silk Tokens). Have 3+ Silk Tokens at the end of your Move phase to win.

5. Official Keywords & Board Positions

Attack: Effect triggers when this card attacks.
Breach: If this destroys a front-row unit, it also deals its damage to the unit directly behind it on the back row.
Death: Effect triggers when this card dies.
Move: Effect triggers when this card moves.
Pacifist: Cannot attack, but can be attacked and deals its Strength in damage when attacked.
Play: Effect triggers when this card is played.
Ranged: Can attack any row (cannot target Leader/Capital if front row has units). Takes no counter-damage when attacking across 2+ rows.
Reincarnate: Can be played from the discard pile once (triggering its reincarnate effect), unless stated otherwise.
Unique: Only 1 copy of this card may be included in a deck.
Adjacent

Next to each other in any direction (up to 3 adjacent friendly cards on a full board: 1 across rows, 2 on same row).

In Front Of

On the front row and directly adjacent to the referenced card on the back row.

Behind

On the back row and directly adjacent to the referenced card on the front row.

Beside

Adjacent to each other on the exact same row.

6. Additional Rules, Leaders/Capitals & Multiplayer

Leaders & Capitals in the Same Deck
  • Max 1 Leader per deck: A deck may never contain more than 1 Leader card.
  • Start with a Leader: you may include at most 1 matching-faction Capital in your 15-card deck (0 Leaders in deck).
  • Start with a Capital: you may include at most 1 matching-faction Leader in your 15-card deck (0 Capitals in deck).
  • Played from hand: grants no resource bonus and costs 0 resources. A Capital counts as a 0-cost Building; a Leader counts as a 0-cost Champion.
  • Win-Condition Impact: Playing a Capital from hand does not let the enemy win by destroying it. Playing a Leader from hand does give the enemy an extra Steel Victory target!
Set Deckbuilding Limits

As of Set 1, each main deck must contain at least 15 cards plus a starting Leader or Capital (planned to expand to 20 in Set 2 and 25–30 later).

Multiplayer Games (3+ Players)

Playable in Teams or Free-For-All. In multiplayer games, all front rows are considered adjacent to all other front rows.
