Legend of silk and steel — Official Rulebook

Complete Game Rules, Card Types, Turn Phases, Keywords & Victory Conditions

1. What is Legend of silk and steel? & The Play Field

Legend of silk and steel is a collectible card game for two or more players. Each player has a Front Row (battle line) and Back Row (support, capitals, buildings), commanding a Leader or Capital and deploying champions and tactics.

2. Card Types & Traits

Capitals (Back Row, Heritage Tokens), Champions (enter unprepared, heal at end of turn), Buildings (limit 1 per turn, cannot move), and Tactics (0-cost one-time effects).

3. Deck Setup & The Four Turn Phases

Minimum 15 cards main deck + 1 starting Leader/Capital, 1 Faction + Neutral/Universal, max 1 Leader per deck, max 3 copies (1 for Unique). Turn phases: Draw → Play → Fight → Move.

4. Three Ways to Win

Steel Victory (0 DEF on enemy Leader/Capital), Legend Victory (Capital Heritage Token threshold), and Silk Victory (10+ resources in Draw phase for 3 Silk Tokens at end of Move phase).

5. Keywords & Positions

Attack, Breach, Death, Move, Pacifist, Play, Ranged, Reincarnate, Unique • Positions: Adjacent, In Front Of, Behind, Beside.

6. Additional Rules & Multiplayer

Max 1 Leader per deck; in 3+ player games, all front rows are adjacent to all other front rows.
